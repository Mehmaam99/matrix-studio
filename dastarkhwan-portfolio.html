01 / The Operational Reality

Why Traditional Desi Restaurants Lose 30%+ Potential Revenue

Traditional Pakistani restaurants face massive call volumes between 8 PM to 12 AM. Counter staff cannot attend every phone call, take notes manually, or update paper menus quickly.

TRADITIONAL BOTTLENECKS

Before Matrix Studio Setup

  • Busy phone lines during dinner rush cause 25–40% missed calls and lost food orders.
  • Physical paper menus get oily, torn, and costly to reprint every time prices fluctuate.
  • Staff struggle to manually explain deals, menu prices, and delivery charges repeatedly.
  • Zero customer database collected — no way to bring customers back on quiet weekdays.
  • Generic unbranded plastic takeaway bags make premium dishes look ordinary.
MATRIX STUDIO UPGRADE

With Automated Dastarkhwan Systems

  • 24/7 WhatsApp AI handles unlimited concurrent customer inquiries with 0 waiting time.
  • Contactless QR Menu allows diners to scan at tables, browse dish photos, and order instantly.
  • Automated table reservation flow captures party size, preferred time, and confirms slots.
  • Organized customer database enables automated Friday deals & birthday discount broadcasts.
  • Custom royal emerald & gold packaging creates unforgettable unboxing for takeaway food.
