# Matrix Studio — Digital Agency Website

Official web presence for **Matrix Studio**, showcasing digital transformation, modern web storefronts, WhatsApp AI automations, creative branding, and business management solutions for local businesses across Pakistan.

## 🚀 Live Demo
- Deployed on Vercel: [matrix-studio.vercel.app](https://matrix-studio.vercel.app)

## 🛠️ Features
- **Design Identity**: Obsidian dark mode (`#080b0b`), neon-lime accents (`#c8ff39`), high-contrast typography and responsive layouts.
- **Bilingual Support**: Instant toggle between English and Roman Urdu.
- **Interactive Solutions Showcase**: 6 business-focused services with dedicated high-res commercial renders and SVG dashboards:
  1. Digital Presence (Websites & Online Stores)
  2. AI & Automation (WhatsApp Assistant & Workflows)
  3. Creative Branding (Identity & Visual Shoots)
  4. Digital Marketing (Promotional Ad Campaigns)
  5. Business Management (CRM & Real-time Operations Dashboard)
  6. Growth Solutions (VIP Loyalty & 5-Star Reviews Engine)
- **Interactive Chatbot Demo**: Scripted industry assistant simulator (Restaurants, Retail, Salons, Clinics).
- **Fast & Lightweight**: Pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript with zero build steps or external dependencies.
